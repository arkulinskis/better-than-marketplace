Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No listing fees, no commission, no ads. You and the buyer settle up in person, however you agree.

Why do I need an account and a location to sell?

So buyers know who they're talking to and roughly how far away you are. They see your display name, your area's name and a distance, never your address or email.

Why 18 and older?

Selling to strangers means meeting up and handling money, so accounts are for adults. We ask for your date of birth when you join and keep only the fact that you confirmed it.

What can't I sell?

Weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco and vapes, medicines, animals, recalled or stolen goods, counterfeits and adult content. The Terms of Use have the full list.

How do I stay safe?

Meet in a public place in daylight or bring a friend, look at the item before you pay, and never pay in advance. Report anything that feels off.

Does it work on my phone?

It's made for your phone. Add it to your home screen and selling something is one tap away.
